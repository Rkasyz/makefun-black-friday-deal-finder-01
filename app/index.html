What the verdicts mean

NEW LOW

The lowest price this product has ever sold for, or the lowest yet for a new model.

STRONG DEAL

A genuinely good price — a big cut, well below what the product usually sells for.

ROUTINE DEAL

A real cut, but one that comes back every sale. Worth buying if you need it; no rush if you don’t.

How these deals were checked

Every deal on this page was found and checked by a person reading the web on 27 Nov 2026: the product page at its store, or a deal source that names the store and its price. The “usual price” is the store’s list or comparison value. Discounts are computed from the two prices shown.

Verdicts are honest on purpose. A price can be an all-time low and still be routine if it returns every Black Friday — the DualSense controller is the example here. A deal can also be real and still sell out, as Dell’s XPS 13 base model did while it was being checked.

Prices change fast during Black Friday week and stock runs out. Always check the store’s price at checkout before you buy — that is the price that counts. FridayFinds takes no payment and earns nothing from these links.
